🌸 Special Letter For Anitha 🌸

Dearest Anitha 🌸,

I may be foolish in your life, but I have never been fake to you. Maybe right now you don't trust me completely, and I know that... but no matter what happens, you will always be truly special to me in my life. Even if one day you forget me, I know for sure that I could never forget you. Even today, I still wonder why that same warmth and heartfelt connection isn't coming from your side, Anitha... but my heart will always care for you the same.

I know I may not have been the best friend to you, but to me, you are the one and only true friend I have ever had. Even people I called best friends were fake to me sometimes, but you were always genuine and never fake. Whenever I get the chance to help you, it feels so special to me — because helping you makes me feel like you are truly my own person in my life. And if tomorrow you achieve greater success than me, remember that I will be the very first person in this world to celebrate and be genuinely happy for you.

I know I overreact sometimes, but even in those moments, thank you for understanding me. In my heart, I always feel that this friendship between us should never, ever break for the rest of our lives.

Sometimes I keep calling you and asking to go out somewhere, for a trip, or for food, and when you can't come, I keep calling... “Naku nuvvu thappa inka evaru leru friends ga...” (I have no other real friends in my life except you, Anitha). That is the only reason I am always calling you. If that ever bothered you or made you feel uncomfortable, I am truly sorry from the bottom of my heart.

It has always been a little dream of mine that one day, you and I could spend some peaceful time together, going on an outing or a trip. I keep holding onto the hope that someday my dream will come true, and I will always be waiting for that day... even though deep down, I sometimes wonder if it will only ever remain a dream.

Finally, a very Happy Birthday to my lovely, sweetest friend! May this year bring you boundless happiness, peace, and endless smiles.

With all my heartfelt care & true emotions,

From your foolish friend Kupendra ❤️
